Who We Are

HARSH CHAUHAN is an independent informational website and online shopping resource created to provide helpful guides, general information, and selected everyday products in one convenient place. Our website covers topics related to utility billing, payment information, account guidance, and other practical consumer resources.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make useful information easier to understand while providing customers with a simple and convenient shopping experience. We create straightforward guides covering billing questions, payment options, account-related information, energy-saving topics, and other commonly searched subjects.

Through our shop, we also offer a selection of products for everyday use, with clear product information, pricing, ordering details, and Cash on Delivery as a payment option where available.

Our Informational Resources

We publish educational and informational content designed to help visitors better understand common utility and billing topics. Our guides may cover subjects such as:

  • Understanding an electricity bill
  • Finding payment information
  • Payment methods and billing questions
  • Account and service information
  • Outage-related resources
  • Energy-saving tips
  • Frequently asked questions
  • General consumer information

Our articles are intended as general information and should not be considered official utility-company customer service.

Our Shop

Our online shop provides selected products with individual product pages containing product descriptions, pricing, images, specifications, and ordering information.

Customers can browse products, add items to their cart, or use Buy Now for a direct checkout experience. Orders are placed using the information provided during checkout, with Cash on Delivery available as the shop's payment method.

Independent Operations & Transparency

HARSH CHAUHAN operates this website independently. We are not Duke Energy, are not an official Duke Energy service center, and do not represent Duke Energy.

We do not process Duke Energy utility payments, access customer accounts, or provide official Duke Energy customer-service functions. References to Duke Energy and other third-party names are provided for identification and informational purposes only.

For official account access, payments, service requests, or utility-company assistance, visitors should use the appropriate official provider resources.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep our website useful, straightforward, and easy to navigate. We work to provide clear information, accurate product details, transparent ordering information, and a convenient experience for visitors and customers.
