Energy Saving Tips to Lower Electricity Use

Practical, low-risk ways to understand consumption and reduce unnecessary electricity use.

Home / Guide

Start with the biggest loads

Heating, cooling and water heating can be significant electricity users. Small thermostat changes, maintenance and avoiding unnecessary runtime can help reduce consumption.

Cooling season

  • Keep blinds or curtains closed during the hottest part of the day.
  • Replace or clean HVAC filters as recommended.
  • Avoid adding unnecessary indoor heat during the hottest hours.
  • Use fans when appropriate to improve comfort.

Heating season

  • Keep doors and windows closed when heating or cooling.
  • Use ceiling fans appropriately for the season.
  • Seal obvious drafts where practical.
  • Maintain heating equipment according to manufacturer guidance.

Track usage

Where available, account dashboards, smart-meter data, usage alerts and home-energy reports can help identify changes before the monthly bill arrives.

Look for wasted energy

Turn off unnecessary lights and electronics, use efficient lighting, and avoid running appliances when they are not needed. The goal is to identify repeatable habits rather than chase one-time savings.
