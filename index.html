Payment and account safety

We explain how utility processes work, but we do not collect passwords, account numbers, card numbers or bank information.

  • Use a verified official domain for account sign-in.
  • Do not share passwords with anyone contacting you unexpectedly.
  • Review the amount and account before submitting a payment.
  • Keep payment confirmations for your records.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay a Duke Energy bill?

Payment methods vary by service area. Our payment guide explains the common workflow, while actual transactions should be completed through official channels.

Why is my electricity bill higher this month?

Usage, weather, billing-period length, rates, charges and household changes can all contribute. Start with the high-bill checklist.

Can I make a payment arrangement?

Some eligible customers may have options such as installment arrangements or other flexibility. Availability and terms can vary by location, account status and current program rules.

Does HARSH CHAUHAN provide customer service for Duke Energy?

No. This is an independent informational resource. We do not access or manage Duke Energy customer accounts.
