Duke Energy kWh Usage Guide: Understanding Electricity Consumption

Learn what kWh means and how to compare electricity use.

Independent informational article · Updated 2026

Home / Blog / Article

What kWh measures

kWh is the unit used to describe electrical energy consumption and is useful for comparing usage between billing periods.

Calculate daily use

Divide total kWh by billing days for a simple average daily-use comparison.

Account for seasons

Seasonal heating and cooling can make one part of the year look very different from another.

Find major loads

Long-running equipment and major appliances deserve attention before small occasional devices.

Read usage patterns

Look for repeated patterns across several months instead of focusing on one number.

Measure changes

After changing a habit or setting, compare later kWh figures to measure the effect.

Quick Question

What is the most useful first step? Start with the current statement and compare the specific figures relevant to the topic rather than relying on assumptions.
