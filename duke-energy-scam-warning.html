Duke Energy Scam Warning: How to Recognize Fake Payment Demands

Identify utility impersonation tactics and protect payment information.

Independent informational article · Updated 2026

Home / Blog / Article

Recognize urgency tactics

Immediate shutoff threats demanding payment during a call are a common social-engineering warning sign.

Watch payment-method warnings

Gift cards, cryptocurrency and unusual payment methods should be treated with extreme caution.

Inspect the website

Fake websites can copy logos and colors, so inspect the real domain.

Do not trust caller ID

Caller ID does not prove that a call is genuine.

Protect credentials

Never disclose passwords, bank logins or full payment-card information to an unexpected contact.

Act quickly after a loss

If money was sent to a suspected scammer, contact the relevant financial institution promptly and preserve evidence.
