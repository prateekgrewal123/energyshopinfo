Duke Energy Outage Preparation: A Household Safety Checklist

Practical preparation for temporary power loss and electrical safety.

Independent informational article · Updated 2026

Home / Blog / Article

Prepare an outage kit

Keep flashlights, batteries, chargers and essential supplies together before an outage occurs.

Keep devices ready

Charge phones and backup power devices when severe weather is expected.

Plan for medical needs

Households relying on powered medical equipment should make a separate continuity plan in advance.

Protect food

Limit unnecessary refrigerator and freezer opening during a short outage and follow food-safety guidance.

Stay away from hazards

Stay away from downed power lines and damaged electrical equipment.

Use official updates

Use official outage information rather than unverified social posts.

After power returns

When power returns, inspect equipment cautiously and follow manufacturer guidance.
