CUSTOMER CARE

30-Day Return Policy

A clear, customer-friendly guide to requesting a return, preparing an item for shipment and understanding the refund process.

30-Day Return Window: Eligible products may be requested for return within 30 days of delivery, subject to the conditions below.

1. Return Window

Customers should contact us within 30 days of the date the order is delivered when they want to request an eligible return.

2. Product Eligibility

Returned merchandise should be in reasonable original condition and must match the item associated with the order.

3. Unused Products

Products that have not been installed, modified or materially used are generally easier to approve for return.

4. Original Packaging

Keep the original box, protective materials, labels, manuals and accessories whenever possible.

5. Damaged on Arrival

If an item arrives damaged, contact us promptly and provide photographs and order details so the issue can be reviewed.

6. Incorrect Item

If the item received does not match the order, contact support before sending anything back.

7. Start a Return

Use the Contact Us page and provide your order number, name, email address and a short explanation of the return request.

8. Return Authorization

Do not ship a return to an address found elsewhere on the internet. Wait for the return instructions supplied for your order.

9. Return Shipping

Return-shipping responsibility may depend on the reason for the return, such as an incorrect or damaged item versus a change of mind.

10. Secure Packaging

Pack the merchandise securely so it is protected during transit. Damage caused by inadequate return packaging may affect eligibility.

11. Tracking

When a return is shipped, keep the carrier receipt and tracking number until the return is resolved.

12. Inspection

Returned products may be inspected to confirm the item, condition and completeness before a refund is approved.

13. Missing Accessories

Missing accessories, parts, manuals or original components may affect the amount or approval of a return when they were supplied with the order.

14. Used or Modified Items

Products that show substantial use, modification, alteration, misuse or intentional damage may not qualify for a standard return.

15. Final-Sale Items

If a product is designated as final sale or non-returnable, that restriction should be disclosed on the product or order information.

16. Exchanges

If an exchange option is offered for a particular item, the exchange will be subject to availability and the applicable return conditions.

17. Refund Basis

Approved refunds are based on the eligible merchandise returned and any applicable deductions described in the order or policy.

18. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if any, may be treated differently from the merchandise price depending on the reason for the return.

19. COD Orders

Because this shop accepts Cash on Delivery, customers should follow the refund instructions provided after an approved return rather than expecting an instant reversal at delivery.

20. Refund Timing

Refund processing begins after the returned merchandise has been received and reviewed. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.

21. Return Not Automatically Approved

Submitting a request within 30 days starts the review process; it does not by itself guarantee approval.

22. Multiple-Item Orders

When an order contains several items, an eligible item may be reviewed separately from the remaining items.

23. Order Information

Keep your order confirmation, order number and the email or phone number used at checkout available when contacting support.

24. Fraud and Abuse

Repeated abusive claims, fraudulent requests or intentional product damage may result in a return being declined.

25. Delivery Evidence

For delivery-related disputes, carrier tracking information and delivery records may be considered during the review.

26. Return Address

Return instructions will be supplied for the specific order. Do not use the business contact address as a return address unless support explicitly instructs you to do so.

27. Warranty Claims

A product defect covered by a manufacturer or product warranty may be handled under the applicable warranty process rather than as a standard change-of-mind return.

28. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when business operations or product categories change. The version published on this website is the current reference.

29. Questions Before Ordering

If you are unsure whether a product is suitable for your needs, review the product page and contact us before placing the order.

30. Contact Us

For a return request or questions about eligibility, visit the Contact Us page and include your order number so the request can be reviewed efficiently.

Need help with a return? Contact us with your order details before shipping anything back.
