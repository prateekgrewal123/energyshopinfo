Duke Energy Pick Your Due Date Guide

Learn the purpose of due-date flexibility and the questions to ask about eligibility and available dates.

Home / Guide

What it means

Duke Energy has described Pick Your Due Date as a way for eligible customers to select a monthly bill due date that better fits their personal schedule and finances.

Why it can help

Aligning a due date with a regular paycheck or household budgeting routine can make it easier to plan recurring expenses.

Eligibility matters

Availability is not universal. Service area, account type and current program rules can affect eligibility and the dates offered.

Questions to ask

  • Which due dates are available?
  • Does changing the due date change the billing cycle?
  • Are there eligibility requirements?
  • When does the new due date take effect?
