Duke Energy Login & My Account Guide

Understand the basic account-access process, password safety, billing history and usage tools without entering credentials on this site.

Home / Guide

Use the official sign-in page

Account login should be completed only on a verified official provider website. Before entering credentials, check the domain and make sure the connection is secure.

What an online account can help with

Depending on service area and account setup, online tools can provide access to billing information, payment history, usage information, notifications and account-management features.

Forgotten username or password

Use the official account-recovery process. Avoid clicking unexpected password-reset links in messages. Navigate to the official site independently when possible.

Account security checklist

  • Use a unique password.
  • Do not share credentials by phone, email or chat.
  • Review account activity if something looks unusual.
  • Use only verified official URLs.
  • Log out on shared devices.

We do not collect credentials

Privacy note: HARSH CHAUHAN does not ask visitors to enter Duke Energy usernames, passwords, payment cards or bank information.
