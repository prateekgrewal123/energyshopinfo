How to Compare Two Duke Energy Bills Step by Step

A structured method for comparing billing days, kWh, charges and balances.

Independent informational article · Updated 2026

Home / Blog / Article

Set up the comparison

Put both statements side by side and record their billing start and end dates.

Compare dates

Compare billing days before comparing totals because different period lengths can distort the result.

Compare kWh

Record kWh and, when useful, compare average daily consumption.

Review charges

Review major charge categories for changes in rates, fees, taxes or other components.

Check credits and balances

Check credits, previous balances and adjustments separately.

Compare daily use

Divide kWh by billing days for a simple daily-use comparison.

Know when to ask for help

If the difference remains unexplained, provide both statements and your notes to the official provider.

Quick Question

What is the most useful first step? Start with the current statement and compare the specific figures relevant to the topic rather than relying on assumptions.
