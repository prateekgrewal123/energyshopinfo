Duke Energy Customer Service Guide

How to prepare for billing, account, payment and outage questions and use verified official channels.

Home / Guide

Choose the right topic

Have a clear description of your issue: billing amount, payment, account access, outage, start/stop service or another concern. This can make the conversation more efficient.

Prepare documents

  • Recent bill
  • Service address
  • Relevant confirmation numbers
  • Dates of payments or service changes
  • Questions you want answered

Protect sensitive information

Never post account credentials, payment-card numbers or bank details in a public form or message. Verify that you are using an official contact method.

For current contact information

Phone numbers and hours can change. Use the official Duke Energy website to verify current contact details for your state and account.
