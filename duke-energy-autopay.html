Duke Energy AutoPay Guide

How recurring utility payments work, what to review and how to avoid payment failures.

Home / Guide

How recurring payments work

AutoPay generally authorizes recurring withdrawals from a payment method according to the provider’s rules. The exact schedule and eligible payment methods should be confirmed in the current account interface.

Before enabling AutoPay

  • Check the correct account.
  • Confirm the payment source.
  • Understand the withdrawal date.
  • Keep enough funds available.
  • Know how to update an expired card or bank account.

If a payment fails

Check the account and payment method through the official site. Avoid immediately submitting duplicate payments without confirming whether the first transaction is pending.

Security

Never provide payment credentials to a third-party website claiming to manage your Duke Energy account.
