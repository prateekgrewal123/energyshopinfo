Duke Energy Bill Assistance & Payment Help

Understand common types of assistance and payment flexibility, while checking current eligibility directly with the official provider.

Home / Guide

Assistance depends on location

Customer assistance programs are not identical everywhere. Duke Energy has published state-specific information for North Carolina, South Carolina, Florida, Indiana, Ohio and Kentucky, and eligibility requirements can differ.

Budget Billing

Budget Billing can help eligible customers manage seasonal changes by creating a more predictable monthly payment amount that is reviewed periodically.

Payment arrangements

Customers who cannot pay a past-due balance in full may have options for additional time or installment arrangements. These are account-specific and should be confirmed with the provider.

Income-qualified and community programs

Some service areas connect customers with state, federal or community assistance programs. Program names, income requirements, application periods and funding can change, so use current official information rather than relying on an old article.

What to prepare before asking for help

  • Current bill and account information
  • Amount currently due
  • Any past-due balance
  • Preferred payment date or amount you can manage
  • Documents required by the specific assistance program

Avoid payment scams

Be cautious of urgent payment demands. Do not send money to an unexpected caller or enter account credentials into a link supplied by a stranger. Verify through an official channel.
